Draft queue

Internal review page. Not indexed, not linked from anywhere on the public site.

Drafts land here after the weekly content agent runs. Each one is a candidate article, not a finished page — nothing here has been fact-checked against the accuracy rules yet.

How to review

  1. Open this page on your phone.
  2. Read the queued drafts below.
  3. Reply in chat: "publish 2 and 4, kill 3" (or however you want to sort them) — no git, no files to touch.
  4. Approved drafts get merged into /articles/, added to the sitemap, and linked from the Guides index. Rejected ones are deleted from this queue.

Standing rules every draft must follow

  • Gross pay only — never taxes, withholding, or take-home pay.
  • Weekly overtime only — never claim ShiftProof calculates daily overtime.
  • Never state or imply a reader is "owed" money, "underpaid," or a victim of wage theft. Use "expected gross," "estimated difference," "compare with your employer."
  • iOS only — never imply Android support.
  • Any legal or payroll claim stays general and cites its source; never state-specific legal advice.
  • If the agent is unsure of a claim, it should flag it here rather than assert it.

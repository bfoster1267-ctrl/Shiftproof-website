Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

What is ShiftProof?

ShiftProof is a native iOS application that helps hourly workers track their shifts and calculate their expected gross pay, including overtime, differentials, and role premiums. It provides scheduling tools and pay estimation features — nothing more.

Your Data Stays on Your Phone

All user data — including shifts, pay rates, workplaces, role premiums, and differential settings — is stored locally on your device only. This data is never collected, transmitted to servers, sold, or shared with third parties. Your phone is the complete and only storage location for your information.

No Account Required

ShiftProof does not require you to create an account. There is no sign-in, no username/password, and no cloud synchronization. Your data remains on your device, under your control.

No Analytics, No Ads, No Tracking

ShiftProof does not:

  • Collect analytics data or usage information
  • Display advertisements
  • Include third-party tracking SDKs
  • Build a profile of your behavior
  • Sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers

Subscriptions and Billing

ShiftProof offers optional in-app subscriptions (Pro tier) managed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Subscription purchases and billing are handled entirely by Apple. When you subscribe, Apple processes your payment and manages your subscription — not ShiftProof. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your Apple ID account settings.

For subscription-related inquiries, contact Apple Support.

External Services

The only external interaction ShiftProof has is with Apple's App Store / StoreKit infrastructure for in-app purchases and subscription management. This interaction is governed by Apple's own privacy policy. ShiftProof does not integrate with or transmit data to any other external service, API, or analytics platform.

Children's Privacy

ShiftProof is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use this app. If you believe a child under 13 has used ShiftProof, contact us immediately using the information below.

Website and App Privacy

This privacy policy covers both the ShiftProof iOS app and this ShiftProof website. The website does not collect personal data, does not use tracking pixels or cookies (except as required by your browser), and does not build profiles. The website is static and does not communicate with any backend server beyond serving its own assets.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of ShiftProof following a policy update constitutes your acceptance of the new terms.

Summary

ShiftProof respects your privacy. Your data is stored locally on your phone, never transmitted, never sold, and never tracked. We do not have a business model that depends on exploiting your data — we make money through optional subscriptions. Your privacy is by design, not by accident.

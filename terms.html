Description of Service

ShiftProof is a scheduling and pay-estimation tool for hourly workers. It allows you to:

  • Enter and track your work shifts on a calendar
  • Set your hourly rates, overtime thresholds, and differential pay rules
  • Calculate your expected gross pay for each shift and pay period

ShiftProof is not payroll software, tax software, accounting software, or legal software.

Important Disclaimer

ShiftProof provides estimates only. The estimated gross pay shown in ShiftProof may differ from your actual employer paycheck due to:

  • Differences in how your employer calculates overtime (daily vs. weekly, state-specific rules, etc.)
  • Taxes, deductions, or other payroll withholdings
  • Employer errors or special pay adjustments
  • Incorrect rate configuration in the app

You must verify your pay against your actual paycheck and your employer's payroll records. Do not rely solely on ShiftProof estimates for financial decisions. If you believe you have been underpaid, contact your employer or payroll department — do not rely on ShiftProof as proof.

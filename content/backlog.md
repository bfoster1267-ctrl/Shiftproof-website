volume target — Brock has ~30 min/week to read drafts on `/drafts/` and reply
in chat with which to publish. See `/drafts/index.html` for the review
workflow and the standing accuracy rules every draft must follow.

to check a real paycheck — not general tax or personal-finance search
traffic. Do not add topics outside this lane just to raise volume; see
"Out of scope" below.

30–60 pages total, not hundreds. Once the 5 cornerstones below are live,
sustainable cadence is closer to 1–2 posts/month, not 2–3/week. Raising
cadence means broadening the audience — that's a decision to make with real
conversion data after the app is live, not by drafting filler now.

## Out of scope — do not draft these

- Anything about federal/state income tax, withholding, W-2s/W-4s, take-home pay, or net pay. This is the one thing ShiftProof explicitly does not calculate; a reader arriving for tax content has a need the app can't serve, and it risks implying accuracy rules don't apply.
- "Am I owed back pay" / wage-theft-framed content of any kind. Hard rule from the product's own accuracy guidelines.
- Daily overtime as something ShiftProof calculates. It doesn't. General educational mentions that daily rules exist in some states are fine (see `weekly-overtime-calculated.html`), but never framed as a ShiftProof feature.
- Android. ShiftProof is iOS only.
- General personal-finance content (budgeting, saving, investing) unrelated to checking a paycheck against shifts worked.

## Sourcing standard

Every factual claim about labor law, overtime rules, or differential
conventions needs a real, checkable source (DOL/FLSA guidance, a state labor
department page, a specific named industry practice) — not a general
assertion. If a claim can't be sourced, cut it or flag it explicitly for
Brock in the draft rather than publishing it unsourced.
